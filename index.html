01 Our story

Started in Jhapa, with the same conviction from day one.

Udaan Engineering and Construction Pvt. Ltd. was founded to close a gap we kept running into as engineers: too many small and mid-size builds were being handed between separate contractors for design, construction, and finishing — with no one accountable for the whole thing.

So we built one company that carries a project the whole way — from soil testing and structural drawings through to the final coat of paint — based out of Jhapa and working across Nepal.

2026

Udaan is founded

Registered in Jhapa with a single site crew and a shared drawing board.

Growing, open for bigger work

A Consultancy services

Independent engineering advice, with or without a contract to build.

Bring us in for the paperwork and planning alone — the drawings, the numbers, the sign-offs — before you commit to a builder.

01

Structural design

Architectural drawings, structural calculations, and municipal map-pass drawings prepared by licensed engineers.

How it works
How it works
  1. Site visit and a discussion of what you need designed.
  2. Architectural drawings prepared to match the site and requirements.
  3. Structural calculations completed by licensed engineers.
  4. Municipal map-pass paperwork filed and handed to you.
02

Cost estimation & feasibility

Site and soil assessment, itemised cost estimates, and feasibility studies before you commit budget to a project.

How it works
How it works
  1. Site and soil assessment carried out on location.
  2. Itemised cost estimate prepared, line by line.
  3. Feasibility weighed against your budget and timeline.
  4. Feasibility report delivered before you commit budget.
03

Project supervision

On-site quality checks, contractor coordination, and progress reporting for clients who need a second set of eyes — even when we're not the builder.

How it works
How it works
  1. Contractor, schedule, and drawings reviewed before work starts.
  2. Scheduled site visits to check quality against the drawings.
  3. Issues flagged and coordinated directly with the contractor.
  4. Weekly progress reports sent directly to you.
B Construction services

Full-service building, one contractor accountable end to end.

Hand us the build itself — foundation, structure, and finishing — carried out by the same crew that did the design.

01

Building construction

Residential and commercial buildings — from a single-family home to multi-storey, multi-unit developments, with reinforced concrete framing built to Nepal's seismic code.

How it works
How it works
  1. Foundation dug and laid.
  2. Structural framing raised, floor by floor.
  3. Reinforced concrete work carried out to Nepal's seismic code.
  4. Finishing completed and the building handed over to you.
02

Roads & infrastructure

Blacktopping, gravel roads, drainage systems, and small public infrastructure works for municipalities and private developments.

How it works
How it works
  1. Site survey and a drainage plan prepared.
  2. Sub-base and gravel layers laid and compacted.
  3. Blacktopping or surface finishing applied.
  4. Final inspection and handover to the municipality or client.
03

Electrical & plumbing

Internal wiring, fixtures, water supply, and drainage lines — coordinated with the structural build, not bolted on afterward.

How it works
How it works
  1. Wiring and pipeline layout planned alongside the structural drawings.
  2. Conduits and pipes laid during the structural build.
  3. Fixtures, switches, and outlets installed.
  4. Full testing and a safety check before handover.
04

Renovation & finishing

Interior finishing, retrofits, and renovation of existing structures — including strengthening older buildings to current code.

How it works
How it works
  1. Existing structure inspected and assessed for condition.
  2. Retrofitting or strengthening carried out to current code.
  3. Interior finishing and fixtures installed.
  4. Final walkthrough and the space handed back to you.
HOW Our process

The same four stages, every project.

Each stage is reviewed and signed off before the next one begins.

01

Survey & scope

Site visit, soil check, and a written scope with an itemised estimate.

02

Design & approval

Structural drawings and, where needed, municipal map-pass paperwork.

03

Construction

Build phase with weekly progress updates and on-site quality checks.

04

Handover

Final walkthrough, punch-list close-out, and documentation handed to you.
